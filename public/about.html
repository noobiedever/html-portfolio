About Me

Hi. My name is Julius Smith. I was born February 23, 1987 in Salisbury, NC. I'm the youngest and only boy of four siblings. I spent my early childhood in Mooresville, NC. I lived in Statesville, NC for the remainder of my childhood. I graduated from Statesville Senior High School in 2005. I went to Winston Salem State University where I received my Bachelor's and Master's in 2009 and 2010, respectively.

After college I was a stay-at-home dad for 10 years. My estranged wife worked for the United States Army. I have three children. Their names are Kaliana(17), Juliana(13), and Julius Jr.(12). I joined the United States Army when I was 32. I was honorably discharged for medical reasons.

As a disable veteran I am now trying to pursue my dreams of being a software developer. I have spent years rehabilitating and healing. I want to be a symbol of what it looks like when you don't give up in life.
